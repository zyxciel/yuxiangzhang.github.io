Biography

I am a speech algorithm engineer at Huawei, working on end-to-end speech translation and speaker diarization with large language models. I received my Ph.D. from the Institute of Acoustics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, where I worked on voice anti-spoofing and speaker recognition under the supervision of Prof. Pengyuan Zhang at the Speech and Intelligent Information Processing Lab. I have published 10+ papers at venues including IEEE/ACM TASLP, ICASSP, INTERSPEECH, and IEEE SPL.

Speaker Diarization with LLM

Weak VAD → LLM (ASR + Turn Detection) → SOT → Clustering · 2025–Present
  • LLM replaces cascaded ASR and diarization pipelines — retains online clustering and unbounded speaker counts
  • Long-form weak VAD avoids sentence loss and over-segmentation; LLM handles ASR + turn detection in SOT format
  • Segment-aware clustering uses LLM-predicted turn boundaries as strong priors (across-boundary → different speakers, within-boundary → same speaker)
  • Real-time streaming output with RAG hot-word correction
Significant improvements across all metrics: reduced WER, speaker confusion, and DER compared to cascade system
Achieved substantial gains over cascade pipeline in WER, speaker confusion, and DER — speaker count error reduced by 45%.
