👋 About

I am a Speech & Audio AI Researcher, working on end-to-end speech translation, speaker diarization with large language models, and voice anti-spoofing. I received my Ph.D. from the Institute of Acoustics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, where I focused on deepfake audio detection and speaker recognition. I have published over 10 papers at venues including IEEE/ACM TASLP, ICASSP, INTERSPEECH, and IEEE SPL, with 300+ total citations.
